## The actual graph and support obstruction

The C188representative684has edges{0,3},{0,4},{1,2},{1,4},{2,3}. Vertices0,...,4form the cycle0-3-2-1-4-0; vertex z=5is isolated. Its induced degrees are(2,2,2,2,2,0), so required crossdegrees are(3,3,3,3,3,5).

Consider any pair Snot containing z. Removing its two vertices from the five-cycle leaves three cycle vertices. They cannot all be adjacent, because a five-cycle has no triangle. Choose a nonadjacent pair u,v among them. Then{z,u,v}is an independent triple disjoint from S. Therefore every such S is forbidden. Conversely every independent triple in this graph contains z: a five-cycle has no independent triple. Hence all five pairs{z,a}, a!=z, satisfy the support requirement, and exactly the other ten pair types are forbidden. The finite consumer verifies this complete support statement directly; the contradiction itself needs only the forward direction.

All ten Bvertices must consequently be adjacent to z, which would give t[z]=10. Saturation instead requires t[z]=5-k[z]=5. This contradiction excludes the whole graph-neighborhood class, after arbitrary relabeling of A.
