# Complete partition-criterion audit; class 671 excluded

**CERTIFIED_RANGE:** all 51 representatives in the pinned Cycle 195 residual were tested under all 720 relabelings against the criterion below. Exactly class 671 matches; the other 50 do not match this criterion. **PROVED at restricted scope:** class 671 cannot be the neighborhood of a selected degree-six root in a simple 17-vertex graph with no K5, no independent four-set, and maximum degree at most seven. The full 17-vertex target and global Ramsey value remain unresolved.

All inputs and outputs are project-local: [complete membership](../data/c196_membership.json), certificate package (historical project reference), saved result (historical project reference), [exact residual](../data/c196_residual.json), and references and credit (historical project reference).

## The sufficient obstruction

Let r be the selected root, A its six neighbors, and B its ten nonneighbors. A has no K4, and B has no independent triple, since r would extend either to a forbidden set. For u in B, its B-nonneighbors form a clique of size at most four. Therefore u has at least five B-neighbors and at most two A-neighbors.

Suppose labels 0–5 of A can be chosen so that:

- 125, 135, 245 and 345 are independent triples;
- 14 and 23 are edges;
- the induced degree k[5] is positive.

Every B type S must meet those four triples. Empty types are impossible. Among singleton/pair types, the only possibilities omitting 5 are 14 and 23: they are the two size-two vertex covers of the four cross-pairs 12, 13, 24, 34. Types containing 5 may be further restricted by other independent triples, which cannot invalidate this necessary partition.

B vertices of a fixed nonempty type S form a clique. The set A minus S has at least four vertices and hence contains a nonedge a,b, because A has no K4. Two nonadjacent B vertices of type S would make an independent four-set with a,b. If S is an A-edge, three B vertices of type S together with S would give K5. Consequently n[14] and n[23] are each at most two.

Population ten now gives

\[
t[5]=10-n[14]-n[23]\ge6,
\]

whereas the maximum total degree at A-vertex 5 gives

\[
1+k[5]+t[5]\le7,\qquad t[5]\le6-k[5]<6.
\]

This is a contradiction. The selected degree-six root is an explicit assumption; no minimum-degree lower bound, added common-neighbor cut, integrality sufficiency theorem, or graph-completion search is used here.

## Why the alternative membership algorithm is complete

The four independent triples force nonedges 12, 13, 24, 34 and all four pairs joining 5 to 1–4. The two required edges are 14 and 23. Since vertex 5 has positive degree and none of 1–4 is its neighbor, edge 05 is required. The only remaining optional edges are 01, 02, 03, 04.

Thus the criterion is exactly membership, after relabeling, in one of the 16 labeled templates with mandatory edges 05,14,23 and an arbitrary subset of 01,02,03,04. Extra independent triples are allowed. This proves both directions of the template characterization rather than inferring it from matching counts.

The primary implementation checks the independent-triple/edge/degree predicate for every permutation of every one of the 51 representatives: 36,720 binary decisions. It retains every satisfying permutation, plus complete positive and negative class lists. The alternate implementation constructs all 16 templates and their 11,520 permutation images, and separately checks every reverse coordinate map against the template set. It compares the exact class lists and all satisfying permutations, not merely their totals.

Both methods find only representative **671**, with eight satisfying permutations. The 50 negative results mean only that this criterion does not apply. They prove neither graph existence nor feasibility of another relaxation. Because the predicate quantifies all relabelings, a negative result for a representative also holds for every member of its orbit.

## Class 671 and its exact base certificate

In the lexicographic unordered-pair encoding, 671 has edges 01,02,03,04,05,14,23. Vertex 0 is adjacent to all five other A vertices; 5 is a leaf. The independent triples are exactly 125,135,245,345, with allowed types

\[
5,\ 05,\ 14,\ 15,\ 23,\ 25,\ 35,\ 45.
\]

Here k[5]=1, so the contradiction is 6≤5. The complete base model keeps all 21 singleton/pair variables, population ten, 13 forbidden-type zero equations, six lower and six upper crossdegree inequalities, all 21 type capacities, and eight A-nonedge avoidance inequalities. There are 55 lifted equations in 62 nonnegative variables.

Subtract the population row from upper:5 + cap:14 + cap:23 and add the 13 forbidden-type zero rows to cancel negative forbidden-column coefficients. The original type-column coefficients are zero; the three selected slack coefficients are one and every other slack coefficient is zero. The combined right side is

\[
5+2+2-10=-1.
\]

Thus M^Ty≥0 and y^Tb=-1. A nonnegative solution of Mx=b would give a nonnegative left side equal to -1, which is impossible. Equality-row multipliers can be negative; the nonnegative slack columns enforce the required signs on inequality multipliers. Signed variables would invalidate this inference.

A separate bitset reconstruction with Fraction arithmetic verifies every incidence row and the certificate. Removing all six zero-weight lower-degree rows and their own slack columns leaves a 49-by-56 system with the same negative margin. The minimum-degree lemma remains a native base-schema dependency, but this exclusion does not use its lower rows.

## Complete orbit removal and stopping point

Every automorphism fixes the unique degree-five vertex 0 and the unique degree-one vertex 5. On the other four vertices it may interchange the two matching edges and independently swap their endpoints. The stabilizer has size 2×2×2=8; the orbit has 720/8=90 members. Forward-edge and adjacency-permutation calculations both evaluate all 720 permutations and match the exact retained C188 membership.

Only that complete orbit is removed: 51 classes /15,322 labeled neighborhoods become **50 /15,232**. The package contains a certificate for every positive class and none for negative classes. Missing classes, missing or duplicate satisfying permutations, missing certificates, altered model support, invalid cone directions, incomplete producers and incomplete orbit membership are rejected by the actual saved-file consumer. It passes 35 selected invalid controls in each of normal, -O and -OO execution. The alternate implementation passes nine selected controls per mode.

The criterion is now exhausted on this residual and every future subset of it. Removing other classes cannot turn a previously negative class into a match. Reopening this identical test requires a changed input estate or changed mathematical criterion, not another scan of the remaining subset.
