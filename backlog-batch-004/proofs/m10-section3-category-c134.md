## 3. Explicit category equivalence check

The source's open-set definition of nowhere dense is equivalent to empty interior of closure. If closure(S) has empty interior, each nonempty open U contains some point outside closure(S); intersect U with the open complement of closure(S) to obtain a nonempty open V disjoint from S. Conversely, if closure(S) contained a nonempty open U, then S would meet every nonempty relatively open subset V of U. Indeed every point of such a V has an open neighborhood inside V, which must meet S by membership in closure(S). This contradicts the source property that U contains a nonempty open V disjoint from S.

For the metric restricted to B_d, an open ball is precisely an ambient Hausdorff ball intersected with B_d; arbitrary unions give the same relative topology. Thus the notions of nowhere dense and meagre used by the source and131 apply to the same topological space. Countable unions, not uncountable unions, are licensed. This elementary equivalence checks a plausible convention mismatch; it is not a fresh proof of H or the source Baire citation.
